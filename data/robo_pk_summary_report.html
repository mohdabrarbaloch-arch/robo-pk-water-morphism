  • Content: short product-demo videos/Reels (~60%) + product photos (~40%), bilingual Roman-Urdu + English captions with a problem→solution hook, a phone-number CTA and an anti-counterfeit warning.

4. Audit — Health Score

54/100
Grade C — Developing
Strong niche content and share behaviour, held back by a very small audience and near-zero reactions.

Strengths

  • Clear, narrow niche (water-pump automation) with a consistent problem→solution message.
  • Video-first cadence — 12 of 20 posts are Reels, the format Facebook favours.
  • Strong share behaviour: 525 shares across 20 posts (avg 26.2) — content clearly resonates enough to be forwarded.
  • Consistent posting (~2.55 posts/week) with bilingual Roman-Urdu + English copy.
  • Good product visuals and a clear phone-number CTA on every post.

Weaknesses

  • Very small audience (91 followers) — reach is capped.
  • Near-zero reactions (32 total) and no public comments — low social proof.
  • Incomplete profile: no website, email or address; not verified.
  • Repetitive creative and hashtags across posts.
  • Phone-number-only funnel — no link, price card or lead form.

Prioritized Growth Recommendations

  1. Convert shares into followers (quick win): pin a "start here" post and add a "follow for water-motor tips" CTA to every Reel.
  2. Complete the business profile (quick win): add website/WhatsApp link, email, address and apply for verification.
  3. Drive comments (quick win): end each Reel with a question ("Aapki motor kitni baar chalti hai?") to lift the algorithm signal.
  4. Double down on winners: the suction-pump and sensor videos get the most plays — make more of those topics.
  5. Test posting times: try Friday and Sunday evening slots and compare plays.
  6. Build a real funnel: WhatsApp click-to-chat link, a price card and a simple lead form.
  7. Paid boost: put a small budget behind the best-performing Reels to grow past 91 followers.
  8. Diversify creative & hashtags: vary thumbnails, hooks and tag sets to avoid fatigue.
  9. Evolve into a trusted local service brand: customer testimonials, before/after installs and a service guarantee.
